LOSTLINK, EXPLAINED

A clear path back
to what matters.

LostLink gives every Chitkara student a secure, respectful way to report, discover and reclaim belongings on campus.

01

Report with useful details

Choose whether your item is lost or found. Add its category, colour, campus zone, date and a public description. A private detail is kept safely away from the listing.

02

Browse and match

Our Phase 1 matcher compares active reports using category, colour, location, date and words in the description. It surfaces the strongest relevant matches first.

03

Verify the rightful owner

Claiming a found item requires specific, non-public answers about it. Incorrect claims are not submitted, and campus administrators can review suspicious activity.

BUILT FOR TRUST

Why LostLink is reliable.

We keep personal contact details off public listings and combine private ownership questions with active moderation. This makes “that’s mine” more than just a claim.

✓ Private details never appear publicly

✓ Multiple ownership questions before a claim

✓ Admins can hide inappropriate reports

✓ Campus-specific zones reduce confusion
